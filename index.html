About

Lifestyle is a wellness technology company incorporated in Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates. We build software that helps people notice how they're doing and take one small, practical step toward feeling steadier — without turning self-care into another source of pressure.

Our approach favors structured, methodology-driven guidance over generic advice: every product we ship is built around a defined framework, delivered through calm, focused interfaces, and paired with a licensed methodology author wherever the subject calls for one.
